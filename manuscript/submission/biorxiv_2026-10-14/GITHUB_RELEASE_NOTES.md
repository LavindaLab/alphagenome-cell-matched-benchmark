# v1.1.0-biorxiv

Revised reproducibility package for *Cell-context matching improves AlphaGenome prioritization of experimentally active regulatory variants*.

This release adds:

- a controlled exact-cell versus nonmatching-context analysis that keeps candidate sets and the ranking rule fixed;
- DNase-only and H3K27ac-only prioritization controls;
- an updated Figure 2 that displays the context and single-track results directly;
- revised biological framing, abstract and discussion;
- machine-readable Supplementary Tables S8-S9 and verification receipts.

The prespecified primary result is unchanged and remains negative. The exact-cell, nonmatching-context and single-track analyses are identified as post hoc throughout the manuscript and supplement.

The release assets include the bioRxiv-ready main PDF, supplementary PDF and machine-readable supplementary-data archive.
