**Search date and cutoff:** 2026-09-13
**Outcome state during search:** held-out Siraj MPRA labels inaccessible
**Purpose:** test the remaining claim immediately before outcome authorization, not establish universal absence

## Searches

Searches combined `AlphaGenome`, `AlphaGenome Atlas`, `MPRA`, `STARR-seq`, `experimental prioritization`, `DNase`, `H3K27ac`, `concordance`, `Siraj`, `fine-mapped`, `AVI`, `external evaluation` and `2026`. Exact titles and DOIs were searched for the AlphaGenome, Atlas, Siraj, Weykopf and Nadal Vidal studies. The official Atlas PDF and primary publication/preprint pages were inspected; newly indexed adjacent projects were also checked.

- Weykopf et al., *Identifying severe COVID-19 risk variants modulating enhancer reporter activity in lung cells*, is a peer-reviewed *PLOS Genetics* article, published 2026-07-17, DOI [10.1371/journal.pgen.1012222](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgen.1012222).

## Newly checked adjacent work

- **AlphaVaR** is a bioRxiv preprint posted 2026-08-21 (DOI `10.64898/2026.08.14.744948`) offering typed AlphaGenome outputs, statistical tests, specificity and transparent multi-criterion prioritization. It is an interpretation framework, not an external five-cell MPRA comparison of fixed Atlas policies.
- **Dutta pan-cancer RegVar** is a medRxiv preprint (DOI `10.64898/2026.05.16.26353369`) integrating AlphaGenome, ENCODE, Roadmap and JASPAR for 6,983 cancer GWAS variants. Its reported validation is regulatory-annotation/GTEx based and explicitly only partly orthogonal; it does not test assay-slot return against independent MPRA labels.
- **Endometriosis prioritization** is a medRxiv preprint (DOI `10.64898/2026.06.27.26356730`) that applies a fixed 0.90 AlphaGenome quantile threshold and modality-breadth tiers to 10,000 variants. It proposes prioritization but does not validate alternative fixed policies on independent functional outcomes.
- **Primary-astrocyte enhancer–gene evaluation** is a bioRxiv preprint (DOI `10.64898/2026.08.14.744988`) evaluating AlphaGenome deletion responses on 2,307 CRISPRi enhancer–gene relations. It is experimentally relevant but asks a relation-level deletion question, not allele-specific SNV direction or fixed-budget MPRA yield.
- **Bauer Lab cross-species evaluation** is currently a public GitHub analysis repository; no associated journal article or preprint was located. It evaluates human/mouse expression and splice-site effects, not MPRA-based experimental prioritization.
- **AlphaGenome encoder fine-tuning on MPRA/STARR-seq** is public code/blog/model-card work. It trains task-specific heads or model weights and therefore does not occupy a zero-shot Atlas policy audit; it does prevent any claim that AlphaGenome has not been adapted to MPRA.

## Occupied claims retained

- AlphaGenome already has cell-matched CAGI5 MPRA analysis, multimodal learned combinations, and eQTL sign accuracy/coverage thresholds.
- Atlas already benchmarks AVI on held-out saturation-genome-editing screens and makes broad noncoding-prioritization claims.
- Weykopf already provides A549 AlphaGenome/STARR-seq validation and reports false negatives.
- Nadal Vidal already provides a cross-locus MPRA miscalibration result.
- AlphaVaR and applied disease studies already provide generic interpretation/prioritization workflows.

## Access-gate conclusion

No located paper, preprint or public analysis jointly uses the held-out Siraj five-cell corpus, exact-cell DNase/H3K27ac scores, global AVI and PIP baselines, fixed assay budgets, locus-diversity accounting, and a prespecified agreement-marker-versus-ranking-policy test. The remaining claim is therefore not occupied as of the cutoff, but novelty risk remains **medium** because Atlas is five days old and follow-on work is appearing quickly.

This search authorizes testing only the narrow frozen claim. A publishability GO still requires the post-result substantive-value gate; nominal significance or generic association cannot establish novelty.
